## Constants

### PI


The ratio of a circle's circumference to its diameter, to `f64` precision.

### E


### abs


Gives the absolute value of an `int`. The smallest `int` has no positive counterpart and comes back unchanged.

