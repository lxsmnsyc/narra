## Group table (194 modules)

Rules: the group follows the module's job. A module that every group imports and that has no phase of its own (`textops`, `hash`, `diagnostics`, `fixes`) goes in driver/. `builtins` is the registry that the std modules fill, so it goes in std/. `buildtarget` picks the target and goes in driver/; `buildconditions` is a checker pass and goes in check/. The dumps of the syntax (`astdump`, `triviadump`) go in syntax/; the dumps of checker results (`declsdump`, `typesdump`) go in check/. A module that no other rule names is a checker module and goes in check/. Only `main` stays at the top.
